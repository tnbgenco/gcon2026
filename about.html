    • Welcome to GCON’26

    The Genco Conference 2026 (GCON’26) will be held on 22nd and 23rd April 2026. The conference with main theme of : “Powering Excellence, Shaping Tomorrow” will be held at the Leo Moggie Convention Centre (LMCC), TNB Headquarters.

    The conference highlights the strength of our internal capabilities while embracing the value of external perspectives. Our plenary sessions will feature TNB Genco’s own subject matter experts, showcasing the depth of experience, innovation, and operational excellence that drive our performance every day.

    Complementing this, our forum sessions bring together distinguished external panelists from across the energy sector and beyond. Their diverse viewpoints will enrich our discussions, broaden our thinking, and help us navigate the evolving challenges of the industry — from energy transition and sustainability to reliability, digitalisation, and market dynamics.

    This conference is more than a gathering — it is an opportunity to learn from one another, strengthen collaboration, and align on the path forward. By combining our internal strengths with external insights, we are better positioned to deliver excellence today while shaping a resilient and sustainable tomorrow.

    Complementing the conference is the Ask The Expert – ‘Gallery Walk’ showcasing the latest technologies used, applications, and solutions from ground sites and reinforce our unwavering commitment to sustained competitiveness and future readiness.
